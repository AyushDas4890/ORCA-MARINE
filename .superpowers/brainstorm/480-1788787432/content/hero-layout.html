How should the hero video be framed?

The video asset itself stays byte-for-byte unchanged — this is about how it sits in the Deep Current layout.

video, full-bleed
ORCA MARINE

A — Full-bleed with dark veil

Video fills the entire viewport, a soft dark gradient veil sits over it so headline text stays legible. Closest to the current site — but with the cyan glow and asymmetric type overlay of Deep Current.

video, glass panel
Headline copy sits beside the video, not on top of it

B — Framed glass panel, split layout

Video lives in its own glassmorphic panel on one side; headline + CTA sit in open space beside it. More editorial, less "video as wallpaper." Video reads as a deliberate object, echoing a sonar/viewport frame.

Headline first
↓ video reveals on scroll

C — Type-first, video reveals on scroll

Opens on bold typographic statement over a dark still frame; the video itself only starts playing full-bleed as the user scrolls past the first fold. Punchier opening, video becomes a payoff rather than instant wallpaper.
